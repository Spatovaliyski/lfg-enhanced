# LFGEnhanced – LFG Filters for WoW Forever

Filters for the **World of Warcraft: Forever** Looking For Group window. Find a tank or healer fast, match your level, and hide the clutter.

## Features

- **Roles:** show only Tanks, Healers or Damage. **Auto** shows only players who fill your group's missing role.
- **Level:** set a range, or show only players near your level.
- **Class:** pick which classes to show.
- **Groups:** show only groups with a spot for your role.
- **Show:** players only, groups only, or both.
- **Hide delisted** greyed-out rows.
- **Playstyle, new-player friendly and Discord** filters.
- **Hide players:** hover a row and click ✕. Unhide anytime.

The filter panel sits next to the LFG window. With all filters off, the list is exactly Blizzard's.

## How to use

Open **Looking For Group → Browse** and set your filters in the panel on the right. The **Filters** button at the bottom shows or hides the panel.

- `/lfg` shows or hides the panel.
- `/lfg reset` turns all filters off.
- `/lfg unhideall` clears hidden players.

## Install

Put the `LFGEnhanced` folder in `_forever_\Interface\AddOns\` and restart the game.

## Notes

- For WoW Forever (interface 16001).
- Can't search group comments; the game doesn't allow it.
- Filtering pauses during boss fights (chat lockdown).
